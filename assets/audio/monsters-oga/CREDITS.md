# monsters-oga - sound credits

Every clip in this folder comes from OpenGameArt.org (OGA) and is released under **CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain dedication** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is legally required for CC0; the authors are listed here anyway as a courtesy.

Licenses were read from the "License(s)" field of each OGA asset page on 2026-09-29 (each page listed exactly one license: CC0). No CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, NC, ND, GPL or game-ripped material is included.

## Modifications applied to every clip

- decoded to mono, 44.1 kHz; stereo sources averaged to mono
- 25 Hz high-pass filter (DC offset / rumble removal)
- leading/trailing silence trimmed (-42 dB threshold, ~4 ms pre-roll), 5 ms fade-in and a short fade-out (8 ms; longer where the source was cut mid-sound)
- peak-normalised to about -1 dBFS, then loudness-matched within each role by RMS (gain reduction of at most 2 dB, or a soft-clip lift of at most 4 dB for quiet transient-heavy clips; the lift is noted per clip)
- encoded as Ogg Vorbis (libvorbis, quality 4)
- some clips are segments cut from longer source files (start/end times listed below)

Role assignment was made from pack labelling/descriptions plus signal analysis (duration, envelope, spectral centroid, spectrograms). The clips were not auditioned by ear.

## Provenance notes

- *8 wet squish, slurp impacts*, *Impact*, *40 wet towel club/pound/hit/attack sounds*, *12 wet towel ... floor sounds* and *Mech Stomp / Step Sound* trace back to qubodup (Iwan Gabovitch) recordings; the two wet-towel packs contain an info .txt naming an older MIT/zlib/BSD multi-license, but the OGA pages state that they are also available under CC0 as of 2024-08-24 ("ignore the txt file"). Both readings are permissive.
- *8 wet squish, slurp impacts* was originally published on independent.nu "Ljudbank" (the OGA page and its comments describe those rights as completely free); OGA lists it as CC0.
- *Mech Stomp / Step Sound* (hc) is a heavily distorted derivative of a qubodup recording, listed as CC0 on OGA.
