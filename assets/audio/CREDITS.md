# Audio credits

The game's sound effects are a mix of code-synthesised sounds (no third-party material) and the third-party sample clips listed here. Every clip was edited for this game (excerpted / trimmed, filtered, level-normalised, converted to mono Ogg Vorbis; a few pitch-shifted or layered) and licence-checked by the researcher who fetched it; per-folder details are in `assets/audio/<group>/CREDITS.md` and `manifest.json`. This file is generated by `node tools/build-credits.mjs` (machine-readable copy: `assets/audio/credits.json`).
