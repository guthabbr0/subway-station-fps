CC0 requires no attribution; the credits are given anyway as good practice. The clips in this folder are derivative works (sliced, trimmed, filtered, level-matched, and in some cases pitch-shifted) of these sources.

Provenance was taken from the file names of the manually downloaded originals in `assets/audio/human-manual-download/` (pattern `NN_<fs|bsb><id>_<title>_<author>_CC0.mp3`). ## Modifications applied to every clip

- Decoded from MP3 (48 kHz mono, or 44.1 kHz for item 46) and resampled to 44.1 kHz mono.
- Sliced to individual vocalisations / hits (silence-gap or onset detection on the RMS envelope, boundaries reviewed on spectrograms; `_analysis/*.png`). `sourceSegment` in `manifest.json` is the range of the original recording that each clip came from (before any pitch shift).
- Leading/trailing silence trimmed (threshold relative to the clip peak).
- DC removed and low end cleaned with a zero-phase high-pass (25 Hz for vocal roles, 40 Hz for foley roles).
- 4 ms cosine fade-in, 12-30 ms cosine fade-out.
- Peak-normalised to about -1.4 dBFS, then loudness-matched inside each role (RMS spread <= ~3 dB) with a small look-ahead limiter (up to 10 dB gain reduction on foley, up to 7 dB on vocals). Levels between roles are NOT balanced; use the `rmsDb`/`peakDb` fields and per-role game gain.
- Encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, quality 4 (libvorbis).

### Extra processing on specific clips (see `pitchShifted` and `notes` in the manifest)

- **Pitch shifting** is tape-style (resampling; duration changes with pitch). `pitchShifted` holds the pitch factor (0.80 = 20% lower).
  - bruteRoar: factor 0.78-0.82 (source: zombie 1/3/4 and Zombie Growl).
  - tyrantRoar: factor 0.62 and 0.65.
  - runnerScream: factor 1.30-1.35 UP. **Experimental**: no source contains a genuine feral screech, so these are pitched-up harsh roar fragments.
  - gib heavy variants (0.70 / 0.75), exploderBurst heavy variants (0.75), impactFlesh (0.70-0.80), zombieStep (0.85), bruteStep (0.50-0.55, plus 1.5 kHz low-pass).
- **Low-pass filtering** (4-6 kHz) on some impactFlesh/zombieStep clips and 1.5 kHz on bruteStep to make them duller.
- spit clips are unprocessed bright crackle bursts (see caveat below).

## Classification method (analysis only, nothing was auditioned)

Roles were assigned from duration, RMS-envelope shape (attack / decay), spectral centroid, band-energy split, autocorrelation pitch and voicing, crest factor, and spectrogram inspection, plus the source titles:

- zombieIdle: voiced (voicing > ~0.75), low centroid (< ~600 Hz) groans, ~0.5-1.5 s.
- zombiePain: short (0.3-0.5 s) grunts, mostly from the "Grunts" item.
- zombieAttack: loud unvoiced/harsh snarl bursts with fast attack.
- zombieAlert: loud sustained roars with formant glide, plus the Zombie Growl (voiced, f0 ~ 200 Hz).
- zombieDeath: fast attack followed by a long (15-40 dB) decay, breathy/low.
- gib / exploderBurst / impactFlesh / zombieStep / spit / bruteStep: strong-transient and burst detection on the foley and bone recordings; impactFlesh and bruteStep are single dull pops/knocks chosen for low spectral centroid.
- `intensity` is relative inside each role (terciles of the source-window RMS), except pitched-down heavy variants, tyrantRoar and bruteStep which are forced to "heavy" and runnerScream which is "medium".

## Known caveats

- Nothing here has been listened to. Roles are educated guesses from analysis; please audition before shipping.
- The zombie recordings are a human voice performance (formants, breath); they read as zombie-ish vocalisations but not inhuman monsters.
- spit: the gore-foley sources contain crunch/crackle, not a smooth wet squirt or hiss. The 3 spit clips are the brightest crackle bursts and are weak.
- impactFlesh / bruteStep / zombieStep are derived by filtering and pitching short foley pops; they are plausible thuds, not real flesh-impact recordings.
- A ~1 ms click in "Zombie 2" at 9.678 s was avoided (tyrantRoar_02 window ends before it).
